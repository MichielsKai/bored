# Changelog

This list also appears in the app (Settings → What's new), so it's written for the people using bored?. The Dutch version is CHANGELOG.nl.md: keep both in step.
Version numbers are **major.minor.patch**. Kai decides whether a release is minor or major.

## Unreleased

### New
- **Streak freezes.** Every 7 days in a row earns a freeze, and you can hold 2. Miss a day and one is used up for you, so a sick day doesn't wipe out your streak.
- **Weekly challenges.** Each week brings a new challenge against your friends, like most time on things you'd forgotten about. The winner gets 500 points.
- **Nudges.** When a friend's streak still needs today's 15 minutes, a bell shows up next to them on the leaderboard. Tap it to give them a nudge.
- **A monthly goal.** Pick how many hours you want each month and follow along with a ring on the home screen. Set it under Stats.
- **Notes on sessions.** Write down what you got done when you log time, or later. Each thing shows its recent sessions with their notes.
- **Edit a session.** Tap a session to change how long it was or its note. Points are worked out again.
- **Pause the timer** for a break, without stopping it.
- **Not this week.** Take something off the wheel for 7 days without retiring it.
- **Search and sort My stuff** once your list gets longer.
- **Undo.** Deleting a session, wish or project step can be undone for a few seconds.
- **Works offline.** The app opens without a connection, and timers and logged time are saved on your device and synced when you're back online.
- **Shortcuts.** On Android, long-press the app icon to spin, continue your last timer or log time.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-11-27

### Fixed
- Updates now reach every device straight away. Before, a phone or tablet could keep showing the old version for hours.
- Signed-in devices no longer shows “1 Jan” for logins from before version 1.0.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-27

### New
- **Points for everything you do.** Every session earns 10 points a minute, boosted by bonuses: your streak (+10% for each day in a row, up to +100%), picking up something you'd neglected (+50% after 2 weeks, +100% after a month), starting the timer straight from a spin (+25%), using the live timer (+20%), and long sessions (+20% for an hour, +40% for two).
- **Streaks.** Do at least 15 minutes a day to keep your streak going. Hitting 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 or 365 days earns bonus points, and so does finishing a project.
- **A points reveal** after you log time, showing every bonus you earned.
- **Friends and a leaderboard.** Add friends by username and compare points this week, this month or all time. Friends only see your points, hours and streak, never what you're doing or what's on your wishlist.
- **What's new**, this screen: see which version you're on and what changed.
- **Quick filters** above the wheel: only spin between things that take under 30 minutes, that suit a low-energy moment, or of a certain kind. Tag your things in My stuff.
- **Project checklists.** Break a project into steps and tick them off. When the wheel picks a project, it shows the next step.
- **High-fives.** See your friends' big moments (streaks, finished projects, achievements) and give them a high-five. You'll get a notice when someone high-fives yours.
- **Weekly recap.** When a new week starts you get last week's highlights: hours, points, your best day, what you spent the most time on, and where you finished among your friends. It's also in Stats any time.
- **50 achievements**, from your first spin to a year-long streak, each worth bonus points. Five of them are legendary and absurdly hard. Good luck.
- **Nederlands.** bored? now speaks Dutch too. It follows your device's language, and you can switch in Settings or on the sign-in screen.
- **A privacy notice** in Settings and on the sign-in screen that explains, in plain words, what's stored and who sees it.
- **Site stats for the admin** in Settings: accounts, active people, hours logged, points, page views and sign-ins, per day and in total. Only totals are counted, never who did what.

### Changed
- **Anyone can sign up.** You no longer need an invite key to create an account.
- The line above a spin result no longer assumes it's evening.
- Sessions from before this version count for points too (without bonuses).
- Up to 8 hours a day earn points, and time added more than 2 days later doesn't, to keep the leaderboard fair.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-27

The first official version.

### New
- **Spin the wheel** to pick a hobby, game or project you already own. Things you haven't touched in a while get a bigger slice.
- **Track your time** with a live timer that keeps running on all your devices, or add it afterwards.
- **Earn new purchases**: every 20 hours on your own stuff unlocks one, and your wishlist waits until then.
- **Invite-only accounts** with an admin, password resets and a list of signed-in devices.
- **Strong security**: carefully stored passwords, protection against password guessing, and dozens of automatic attack tests before every update.
- **Add to your home screen** for an app-like experience, with sounds and confetti along the way.
